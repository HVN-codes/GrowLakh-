## The Problem

Millions of gig workers and low-income families with volatile income are locked out of wealth creation. Hidden bank penalties, overdraft fees and high-interest loans drain their money, and they cannot build assets.

**Who is affected:** informal-economy workers, delivery partners (e.g. Uber/Zomato), small freelancers, and lower-income rural-urban families living paycheck-to-paycheck.

**Pain points**

- **Wealth degradation:** capital lost to hidden fees, penalties and payday loans.
- **The paystub barrier:** no fixed income history means no access to mutual funds, housing or institutional micro-loans.
- **High failure risk:** no low-risk way to test a business idea before moving from gig labour to ownership.

---

## Our Idea

GrowLakh is an automated financial mobility platform that turns survival budgeting into active wealth generation.

It helps users to:

1. **Stop the leaks:** find and flag hidden fees in their transaction history.
2. **Earn faster:** pick up zero-capital local micro-gigs and sponsored financial-literacy courses that add to a locked milestone fund.
3. **Unlock at ₹5 Lakh:** reaching the milestone opens a path-selection screen.
4. **Deploy with lower risk:** start automated SIP investments, or launch a pre-validated local micro-business after collecting customer pre-orders (for example through a localized WhatsApp catalog and UPI link).

### Example user journey

A delivery driver removes ₹1,500/month in hidden fees → earns extra through in-app modules → reaches the ₹5 Lakh milestone → chooses a "Cloud Kitchen" blueprint → collects pre-orders from neighbours via a UPI link → launches the business.

---

## Key Features

- **Predatory Fee Audit:** shows where money is leaking.
- **Alternative Gig-Credit Profile:** turns irregular deposits into a verifiable trust rating.
- **₹5 Lakh Threshold Matrix:** moves users from saving to asset deployment.
- **Zero-Capital Micro-Gig Station:** in-app ways to earn.

### What makes it different

Budget apps (like Walnut or Splitwise) only track spending. Stock brokerages need financial literacy and starting capital. GrowLakh connects **saving, earning and launching** in one loop.

## Impact & Scalability

- **Impact:** faster path to financial independence for lower-income users. Our target is to cut systemic debt-trap risk by up to 40%.
- **Beneficiaries:** gig workers, street vendors, rural micro-entrepreneurs, and unbanked communities.
- **Scalability:** partnerships with CSR bodies, micro-finance institutions and gig-delivery aggregators through standard REST APIs.

---

## Roadmap

- **Phase 1: Hackathon MVP:** bank statement parsing engine, milestone dashboard, and mock business runway planners.
- **Phase 2: Post-hackathon:** live bank API integrations, alternative credit scores, and AI marketing generators.
- **Phase 3: Go-to-market:** corporate sponsorship onboarding for Learn-to-Earn workflows.
